# Legacy and intent

Meta-GBA is deliberately a feature edition rather than a premium or paid tier. That decision supersedes earlier premium wording. The project is about preserving a hands-on, user-controlled workflow: inspect the input, make a small change, test it, and keep the original safe.

“Git-FED's way or the highway” means the project favors versioned, reviewable, local-first work. It is not permission to bypass platform rules, copyright, access controls, or safety requirements.

The project is personal/experimental and non-commercial. If a request conflicts with that boundary, it is out of scope.
