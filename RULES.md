# Rules

1. Personal or experimental use only; no commercial use.
2. Patch-only. Never distribute a ROM or package a patch with a ROM.
3. “Not allowed” means not discussed here: do not infer permission from silence.
4. Work Git-FED's way: retain full control for security and safety, review diffs, and keep changes reversible.
5. Prefer editing an existing, clearly identified project over adding unrelated files.
6. Make one focused change at a time; back up first and test in an emulator.
7. Do not automate bulk edits or mass patching.
8. Do not remove attribution, licenses, provenance, or safety notes.
9. Do not use proprietary or unlicensed assets; suggest an original game with legal assets when that is the safer alternative.
10. Stop when the target version, legal status, or requested scope is unclear.
