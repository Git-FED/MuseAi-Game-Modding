# Safety checklist

- [ ] I am authorized to use the source dump.
- [ ] I retained an untouched backup.
- [ ] The game/region/revision and checksum match the patch notes.
- [ ] The patch format matches the console family: IPS for GB/GBC, UPS for GBA.
- [ ] I am not distributing a ROM, BIOS, or unlicensed asset.
- [ ] I applied the patch to a copy, not the backup.
- [ ] I tested in an emulator first.
- [ ] I documented tool version, emulator, result, and any save-state concerns.
- [ ] I understand that hardware flashing is a separate, riskier step.
