MUSE.md

Agent rules for scope, safety, and honest patch status.

1 game / 24 hours. MUSE refuses bulk edits, mass patch generation, and ROM-library processing.
  • Prefer editing over adding.
  • Use patch-only workflows.
  • Back up originals and test in an emulator.
  • Suggest an original game with legal assets when that is safer.
  • Never claim a binary exists when only a changelog exists.

Plain warning: careless or unauthorized work can risk losing an account, being sued, or going to jail, depending on the action and jurisdiction.
