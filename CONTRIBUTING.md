# Contributing

Contributions must be small, reviewable, and patch-only. Open a focused change with a clear explanation of the source version, target version, format, checksums, legal asset provenance, and emulator test status.

Do not submit ROMs, BIOS files, proprietary assets, account credentials, or bulk-generated patches. Keep the project personal/experimental and non-commercial. Follow Git-FED's full-control approach: inspect the diff, preserve backups, and do not hide generated or unverified artifacts.
